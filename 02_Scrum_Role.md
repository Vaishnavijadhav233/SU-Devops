# 02 Scrum Role

## Scrum Team Structure

| Role | Assigned Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Product Owner | Requirements, backlog priority and stakeholder needs |
| Scrum Master | Scrum meetings, impediments and team support |
| Development Team | Design, coding, testing and documentation |

## Scrum Roles and Responsibilities

### Product Owner

- Collect requirements from stakeholders.
- Prepare and prioritize the Product Backlog.
- Clarify user stories.
- Review completed work.

### Scrum Master

- Facilitate Scrum meetings.
- Help remove blockers.
- Support Agile practices.
- Maintain clear team communication.

### Development Team

- Design UI and database.
- Implement features.
- Perform testing.
- Maintain documentation and demonstrations.

> Team member names can be added after the group finalizes the allocation of roles.
